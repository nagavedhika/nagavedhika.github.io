# Day 3 — Team Formation, Collaboration & Rapid Prototyping

Day 3 was about transforming a group of individuals into a functioning team.

Our Beta Team was formed, and an Among Us-inspired icebreaker helped break the remaining tension within the group. We then participated in observation challenges that encouraged active listening, discussion, and collaborative thinking.

The Marshmallow Tower Challenge pushed us to work under constraints using simple materials such as spaghetti sticks, tape, thread, and marshmallows. Rather than focusing only on the final tower, the challenge taught us about communication, experimentation, iteration, and adapting when an idea does not work.

Through these activities, we began understanding each other's strengths and working styles.

## Daily Reflection

> “We stopped being just people in the same team and started becoming a team that could work together.”

## Key Takeaway

**Shared Challenges + Active Listening = Trust & Alignment**
