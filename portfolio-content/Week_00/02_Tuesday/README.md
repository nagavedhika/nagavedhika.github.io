# Day 2 — Self-Awareness & Diverse Perspectives

Day 2 focused on understanding ourselves and appreciating how differently people perceive the world.

Through 16 Personalities and Web Stories, we explored different personality types, cognitive patterns, and ways of thinking. This helped us recognize that people can approach the same situation from completely different perspectives.

We also explored Zen Pencils, selecting stories and comics that connected with our personal, academic, or lived experiences. Presenting these stories encouraged us to look beyond the content itself and connect narratives with our own personalities, experiences, and values.

The activity demonstrated how the same story can create completely different interpretations depending on the person experiencing it.

## Daily Reflection

> “We discovered that diversity is not just about different backgrounds — it is also about different ways of thinking.”

## Key Takeaway

**Perspective is shaped by how we uniquely interpret the world.**
