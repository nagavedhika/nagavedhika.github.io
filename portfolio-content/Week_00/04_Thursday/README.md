# Day 4 — Intentional Technology & Future Vision

Day 4 introduced us to the broader vision of technology, artificial intelligence, and purposeful problem-solving.

The ProtoSem Inauguration Ceremony at SKT, KCT provided valuable insights from the distinguished guest speaker and helped us understand the larger purpose behind the program.
