# Day 1 — Starting Conversations & 360° Interaction

The first day focused on breaking initial barriers and connecting with people from different backgrounds and disciplines.

We began with personal introductions and cross-disciplinary conversations, which helped us step outside our comfort zones and interact with peers we had never met before.

The Rock-Paper-Scissors activity turned networking into an energetic and engaging experience. Moving across teams and interacting with different people gradually reduced our hesitation and encouraged open communication.

The day then shifted from connecting with people to observing the world around us. We were introduced to the idea of identifying real societal problems based on everyday experiences rather than assuming problems from a distance.

## Daily Reflection

> “We started by getting to know people — and then started looking at the world around us differently.”

## Key Takeaway

**Connection + Observation = The Start of Problem-Solving**
