# Week 0 Overview

Week 0 marked the beginning of our ProtoSem journey. It was less about immediately building solutions and more about understanding people, ourselves, our surroundings, and the purpose of technology.

Across four days, we moved from being strangers within a diverse cohort to becoming a connected team. Through icebreakers, self-awareness activities, collaborative challenges, problem-identification exercises, and AI-focused sessions, we began developing the mindset required to approach real-world problems differently.

## Week 0 Synthesis

### From Strangers to a Connected Team

Week 0 represented a clear evolution in the way we interacted and worked together:

**Strangers → Self-Awareness → Teamwork → New Perspectives → Connected Team**

What began with simple introductions gradually developed into collaboration, shared understanding, and a stronger sense of purpose.
