# Week 0 Synthesis

## From Strangers to a Connected Team

Week 0 represented a clear evolution in the way we interacted and worked together:

**Strangers → Self-Awareness → Teamwork → New Perspectives → Connected Team**

What began with simple introductions gradually developed into collaboration, shared understanding, and a stronger sense of purpose.

### What We Took Away

**01 — Connection**
We learned to interact with people beyond our comfort zones and build meaningful connections across disciplines.

**02 — Problem Discovery**
We began training ourselves to observe everyday situations and recognize real societal problems worth solving.

**03 — Self-Awareness**
We gained a better understanding of different personalities, cognitive styles, and the influence of individual perspectives.

**04 — Collaboration**
We experienced how different ideas, strengths, and approaches can come together to achieve a shared goal.

**05 — AI Awareness**
We learned that AI and technology become truly valuable when they are applied intentionally to specific problems and contexts.

### Final Reflection

> **“ProtoSem didn't just teach us new concepts. It taught us how different people can connect, think differently, and create together.”**

Week 0 laid the foundation for everything that followed. Before learning how to build solutions, we first learned how to connect, observe, understand, collaborate, and think with purpose.
